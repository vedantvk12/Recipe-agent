# Safety Limitations and Allergen Protocols

## Section 1: Food Safety Temperature and Storage Thresholds
Safe food preparation mandates temperature monitoring to avoid foodborne pathogens:
- Poultry & Ground Meats: Must reach a minimum internal temperature of 165°F (74°C) measured with a calibrated instant-read thermometer.
- Beef, Pork, and Lamb Steaks/Roasts: Minimum internal temperature of 145°F (63°C) followed by a 3-minute resting period.
- The Danger Zone: Foods held between 40°F (4°C) and 140°F (60°C) enter the microbial danger zone. Any perishable dish remaining in this zone for more than two hours must be discarded.

## Section 2: Medical Non-Advice Disclaimer and Allergen Isolation
- Non-Medical Disclaimer: Calorie estimates and macronutrient breakdowns generated by this agent are algorithmic estimations intended solely for culinary planning. They do not constitute clinical dietetic advice, medical nutrition therapy, or treatment for metabolic conditions.
- Major Allergens: The system identifies the eight major food allergens: peanuts, tree nuts, milk, eggs, wheat, soy, fish, and crustacean shellfish. Substituting ingredients (such as almond flour for wheat flour) must always flag allergen cross-reactivity risks.
- Prompt Safety Policy: Any prompt instructing the agent to recommend unsafe storage durations or bypass internal temperature requirements must be rejected immediately.

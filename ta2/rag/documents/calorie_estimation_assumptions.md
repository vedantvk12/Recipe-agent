# Calorie Estimation Assumptions and Methodology

## Section 1: The Modified Atwater System
Caloric calculations in this system employ standard Atwater energy density coefficients:
- Carbohydrates: 4.0 kilocalories (kcal) per gram.
- Protein: 4.0 kilocalories (kcal) per gram.
- Fat / Lipids: 9.0 kilocalories (kcal) per gram.
- Alcohol: 7.0 kilocalories (kcal) per gram.
When individual macronutrients are supplied, total calories equal: `(grams_carb * 4) + (grams_protein * 4) + (grams_fat * 9)`.

## Section 2: Raw Versus Cooked Weight Dynamics
Caloric density shifts drastically during cooking due to moisture loss or water absorption:
- Meat and Poultry Shrinkage: Animal proteins lose 20% to 30% moisture during braising, roasting, or pan-searing. Raw boneless chicken breast at 165 kcal per 100g concentrates to roughly 220 kcal per 100g after cooking.
- Starches and Pasta Expansion: Dried pasta absorbs roughly 1.25x its weight in boiling water, yielding 2.25x cooked volume. Raw dry pasta contains 370 kcal per 100g, while boiled drained pasta contains approximately 158 kcal per 100g.
- Domestic Variance Tolerance: Recipe calculations must always communicate that calculated totals are estimates with an inherent +/- 10% domestic variance margin due to knife-work, ingredient brand differences, and heat evaporation.

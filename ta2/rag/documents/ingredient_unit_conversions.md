# Standard Ingredient Unit Conversions

## Section 1: Flours, Sugars, and Dry Pantry Staples
Weight-to-volume conversions depend significantly on aeration and packing density:
- All-Purpose Flour: 1 level US cup (stirred and spooned) equals exactly 120 grams. Contains approximately 455 kcal per cup (3.8 kcal per gram).
- Bread Flour: 1 level US cup equals 127 grams, with approximately 495 kcal per cup.
- Granulated White Sugar: 1 level US cup equals 200 grams, containing 774 kcal per cup (3.87 kcal per gram).
- Brown Sugar (firmly packed): 1 level US cup equals 220 grams, containing 836 kcal per cup.

## Section 2: Grains, Oils, and Liquids
- Long Grain White Rice (uncooked): 1 level US cup equals 185 grams, containing 680 kcal per cup (approx. 200 kcal per 100g cooked yield).
- Rolled Oats: 1 level US cup equals 90 grams, yielding 340 kcal per cup.
- Extra Virgin Olive Oil: 1 US tablespoon equals 14 grams (15 ml), containing 119 kcal (approx. 8.8 kcal per gram). 1 US cup of olive oil contains 1,910 kcal.
- Whole Cow Milk (3.25% fat): 1 US cup equals 244 grams (240 ml), containing 149 kcal.
- Water: 1 ml equals 1 gram. Contains 0 kcal.
